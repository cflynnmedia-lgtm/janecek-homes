Built Around the Water

Riviera Lake Havasu.

One of the few communities built on Lake Havasu itself. Janecek has secured more than fifty homesites across the Riviera Lake Havasu neighborhoods, from Marina View, Wren Cove, and The Ridge to Black Rock and Flat Irons, with the largest presence in the flagship neighborhood, Sixty-Seven.

The Communities

several neighborhoods.

Riviera Lake Havasu unfolds along the water as a collection of distinct communities. Janecek holds homesites across several of them. Start with the overview, then step into each one.

Riviera's Newest

Sixty-Seven.

Inquire About Homesites

Sixty-Seven at Riviera Lake Havasu is a stunning neighborhood of 77 homesites terraced above Lake Havasu, offering sweeping views of the lake, surrounding mountains, and natural desert landscape. With dramatic elevation changes, desert hillsides, and nearby canyons, Sixty-Seven provides a unique setting with a wide variety of homesite options and unforgettable views in every direction.

Homesites in Sixty-Seven range from approximately 15,000 square feet to nearly an acre, with opportunities for both single-level and two-story home designs. The neighborhood’s west to southwest orientation allows homeowners to enjoy panoramic lake views, mountain backdrops, and incredible Arizona sunsets from one of the most elevated and scenic locations within Riviera Lake Havasu.

On the Water

Wren Cove.

Inquire About Homesites

Set along the washes and ridgelines in the heart of Riviera Lake Havasu, Wren Cove offers a rare blend of privacy, natural beauty, and elevated desert living. Each homesite is positioned to capture its own unique perspective, from intimate views of Wren Cove and the surrounding desert to sweeping lake, mountain, and sunset vistas.

Located near the shoreline of Lake Havasu and just inside the main gate of the community, Wren Cove provides convenient access to Lake Havasu City while still feeling tucked away in a peaceful, scenic setting. The neighborhood is surrounded by some of the area’s most recognizable natural landmarks, including Copper Canyon, Skiers Cove, Cup Cake Mountain, and the pristine turquoise waters of Lake Havasu.

Homes in Wren Cove are thoughtfully designed for comfort, function, and the Arizona lifestyle, with spacious homesites, single-level living, and room for RV garages that make it easy to explore the Southwest. With close proximity to Riviera Marina, trailheads, desert open space, and the water’s edge, Wren Cove is an ideal place to enjoy both everyday relaxation and outdoor adventure.

For those seeking luxury living surrounded by lake views, mountain backdrops, and the natural beauty of the desert, Wren Cove offers a truly exceptional setting to create lasting memories.

The Setting

Black Rock.

Inquire About Homesites

Black Rock at Riviera Lake Havasu is a distinctive neighborhood set between two natural rock canyons in the heart of the community. Beginning at one of the highest points within Riviera Lake Havasu, the streets and homesites terrace approximately 150 feet down the hillside toward the water’s edge, creating dramatic elevation changes and exceptional views throughout the neighborhood.

Black Rock features 61 carefully planned homesites designed for custom single-family homes. Lot sizes range from more than 10,000 square feet to nearly an acre, offering a wide range of opportunities for homeowners looking to design and build from the ground up. The natural terrain allows for unique architectural possibilities, including two-story homes, split-level designs, and walkout basement layouts along the canyon edges.

With its varied homesite sizes, dramatic topography, and a mix of lake, mountain, desert, and canyon views, Black Rock offers one of the most unique and diverse living environments within Riviera Lake Havasu.

Among the Foothills

Flat Irons.

Inquire About Homesites

The Flat Irons neighborhood captures some of Riviera Lake Havasu’s most dramatic desert and mountain views. Set along the eastern edge of the community near untouched BLM land, it offers a rare blend of privacy, open space, and natural beauty. From the Aubrey Hills to the surrounding desert and the Flat Iron mountains, the views create a setting unlike anything else in Lake Havasu.

Homesites here are designed for spacious desert living, averaging nearly 12,000 square feet with large, level lots and flat, single-level building pads. Many also allow for two-story homes, giving residents the chance to maximize their living space and capture even more of the surrounding scenery. With recreation, open desert, and endless views right outside the door, Flat Irons is a truly unique place to build within Riviera Lake Havasu.

A Look Around Flat Irons
Closest to the Water

Marina View.

Inquire About Homesites

The Marina View neighborhood at Riviera Lake Havasu offers unforgettable lakefront views unmatched in Arizona. Of all the neighborhoods in the community, Marina View is situated closest to the Havasu Riviera State Park and Marina. You can walk out your front door and be on the water in minutes, allowing homeowners to achieve an active lifestyle found nowhere else in the state.

The Marina View neighborhood includes 74 single family homes terraced above Lake Havasu with dramatic sweeping views. The lots range in size, from an average of 9,100 square feet to larger lots over 15,000 square feet. Most lots have multiple levels and support a wide variety of homes with large garages and all the extra space you could want.

Marina View also includes the Sunset Ridge Arizona townhome project. This pocket of 17 two-story townhomes is currently being designed to create a lifestyle and living option unlike any other in Lake Havasu. This is an excellent alternative for prospective buyers who want the gorgeous views without the requirement to maintain a yard.

With its varied lot sizes, building configurations, and an endless variety of lake, mountain, and canyon views, along with its proximity to the Marina, Marina View offers a lakefront lifestyle and is one of the most unique and diverse neighborhoods in Riviera Lake Havasu.

Above It All

The Ridge.

Inquire About Homesites

The Ridge neighborhood is draped along the top of a series of natural ridges in the desert surrounding Riviera Lake Havasu. View? Your choice. From city views or stunning panoramic lake views to awe-inspiring desert and mountain views, the choice is yours in The Ridge neighborhood. You can come home to the landscapes and vistas that call to you.

The Ridge neighborhood consists of 65 terraced homesites in a unique configuration. Rising high above the rest of the community, the Ridge features stunning views of Lake Havasu, sweeping mountain and desert views, and even incredible views of Lake Havasu City itself. The neighborhood features a mix of large, level homesites and multi-level lots, allowing flexibility in choosing the perfect single- or multi-level homes with spacious boat and RV garages.

Featuring a neighborhood pool facility, convenient access to hiking and exploring the natural desert, and quick and convenient access to the Havasu Riviera State Park and Marina, the Ridge is the perfect place to call home. Discover the best Arizona luxury homes the state has to offer.
