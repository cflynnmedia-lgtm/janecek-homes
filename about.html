Who We Are

Built on one simple commitment.

As one of Lake Havasu City's premier custom home builders, Janecek Homes has been building beautiful, quality custom homes for over 30 years. Melding quality craftsmanship and livable luxury, we have set the standard that discerning high-end homeowners desire to live in, and other home builders aspire to build.

Three decades on, that standard has carried every project, from intimate single-family residences to the multi-residence Villas at the Foothills, and now to the 50 exclusive homesites we've acquired within Riviera Lake Havasu.

And it has always come back to the same local people, on every build. The faces behind that work are the ones worth introducing.

Where It Began

It started with Bob.

Before the Riviera homesites and the custom estates, there was a framing crew on a Lake Havasu lot and a builder named Bob Janecek. He built every home like he'd have to answer for it, and set the standard the company still measures itself by.

From 1990 through 2025, Bob and Justin raised more than two million square feet of under-roof residential building in Lake Havasu City alone. A father and a son, the same trades, the same town, decade after decade.

Justin learned it at his father's side, on real sites and real framing. That is the inheritance behind every home Janecek builds today.

The Process

From vision to keys.

Building a home is one of the most significant, and often most stressful, journeys a client can take. We've refined a process to ensure it's neither.

01Consultation

A thoughtful conversation

It starts with a thoughtful conversation. We get to know you, your vision, your lifestyle, your priorities. Where you spend your mornings. How you entertain. What you've always wanted in a home and never had. This isn't a sales call. It's the foundation everything else is built on.

02Design

Vision becomes form

Our in-house exterior and interior design team translates the conversation into form. Floorplans evolve. Elevations get tested against the actual site. Material palettes come together. You're involved at every decision point, never bypassed, never overwhelmed.

03Team

The right people, assembled

We assemble a trusted team of local professionals around your project. The same subcontractors we've worked with for years, masons, framers, electricians, finish carpenters, who know our standard and meet it consistently. No revolving door. No subcontractor lottery.

04Build

Precise, managed execution

Precise execution with the experienced and reliable subcontractors who have built our reputation. Weekly walks. Clear communication. The kind of project management that turns a year of construction into a milestone you actually enjoy reaching.

05Delivery

The keys, and what comes after

A finished home, walked through alongside you. Punch lists actually closed out. Warranty terms you can read in a single page. A relationship that continues long after the keys change hands, because Lake Havasu is a small town, and a builder's reputation is the only thing that lasts.
