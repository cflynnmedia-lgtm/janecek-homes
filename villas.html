More than twenty networks of off-road trails wind through sand dunes, canyons, and open desert, all within driving distance. Hike, off-road, ride dirt bikes, ATV, or UTV, and explore at your own pace. At The Villas, the desert is your backyard playground.

High in the

The Villas is a planned community within the larger Havasu Foothills, its 158 homesites built out alongside The Arroyos and the phases beyond. Close to town, yet opening straight onto open desert.
