Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Janecek Homes, Inc. ("Janecek Homes," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit janecekhomes.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1.Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you give us directly, and information collected automatically as you browse.

Information you provide

When you fill out a contact or inquiry form, request information about a homesite or community, or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Details you choose to share about your interest, such as a community, timeline, budget range, or notes about the home you have in mind

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our service providers may automatically collect limited technical information through cookies and similar technologies, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits. This information helps us understand how the Site is used and improve it.

2.How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the information you request
  • Follow up about homesites, communities, or custom build opportunities
  • Communicate with you about your project and our services
  • Maintain, operate, and improve the Site
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information.

3.How We Share Information

We share personal information only in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. We may share information with trusted third parties who perform services on our behalf (for example, website hosting, form processing, email delivery, or analytics) and who are obligated to protect it.
  • Sales partners. If your inquiry concerns a specific community (such as Riviera Lake Havasu or The Villas at the Foothills), we may share your information with the relevant sales representative or partner to respond to you.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of Janecek Homes, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers. If Janecek Homes is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4.Third-Party Services

The Site uses third-party services that may collect information under their own privacy policies. These may include web fonts, embedded video, mapping, and analytics providers. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites we link to.

5.Cookies & Your Choices

Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent; however, some parts of the Site may not function properly without them. Most browsers also allow you to delete cookies already stored.

6.Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect against loss, theft, and unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7.Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

8.Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9.Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, or delete it, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, contact us using the information below. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

10.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
